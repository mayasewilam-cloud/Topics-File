# 🎓 Student Guide: Graduation Project Topic Submission

Welcome, Biotechnology senior students! This guide will assist you in completing your graduation project topic registration.

---

## 🔗 How to Access the Form

Open the official registration link provided by your graduation project coordinators (hosted on GitHub Pages).

You can access the form from any mobile browser, tablet, or laptop.

---

## 📝 Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Find Your Record
- In the **Search Your Name or Student ID** box, start typing your 6-digit Student ID (e.g. `245075`) or your first name.
- Select your record from the dropdown list.
- Your official **Venue**, **Field**, and **Assigned Supervisors** will appear automatically in the blue allocation card.

### 2. Verify Your Venue & External Supervisor
- Your assigned venue and external supervisor are locked by default to protect official committee allocations.
- If an official change occurred, click **✏️ Edit** to update them. Your row will be highlighted for committee review.

### 3. Enter Your Graduation Project Topic Title
- Type your final approved topic title.
- **Topic Quality Guidelines:**
  - Must be **at least 15 characters** and **at least 3 words**.
  - Must describe the scientific work accurately (e.g., *“Evaluation of Secondary Metabolites from Endophytic Fungi against Multi-Drug Resistant Bacteria”*).
  - ⚠️ Generic words like *"test"*, *"TBD"*, *"pending"*, *"cancer"*, or *"lab work"* are flagged by the audit scanner.

### 4. Provide Your Contact Phone Number
- Entering a valid Egyptian phone number (e.g., `01xxxxxxxxx`) is **mandatory**.
- Submissions cannot proceed without a contact number.

### 5. Log Supervisor Meetings
- Enter the number of meetings conducted with your External Supervisor and Internal Doctor.
- Specify the academic position of your External Supervisor (e.g., *“Professor of Biochemistry, NRC”*).

### 6. Save & Synchronize
- Click **💾 Save & Synchronize Topic**.
- You will receive a green confirmation banner:
  > **Topic Registered Successfully!** Your topic, supervisor interactions, and contact details have been directly recorded into the official Topics File in Google Sheets. (If revising an already submitted topic, your record will be highlighted in **YELLOW** for coordinator review).
